Frequently asked questions

What is Livekawsay?

Small-group local experiences in Lima, Peru, hosted by locals, so you feel part of the place instead of just visiting it. Kawsay means “to live” in Quechua.

What is a pichanga?

An informal pickup football match — a core part of neighborhood life in Peru. You play one with a local Lima team.

How much does it cost?

The Peruvian Pichanga is US$60 per person (1 hour of play, ~30 min transfer and a local beer). The cocktail class is US$70 per person, 1 hour.

Do I need to speak Spanish or be good at football?

No. We host in English and Spanish, and every level is welcome.

Which cocktails will I learn?

Pisco sour, Machu Picchu (a layered pisco cocktail) and chilcano (pisco, ginger ale and lime).

Can tourists play football in Peru?

Yes. With the Peruvian Pichanga you play football (soccer) with a local team in Lima. No team or contacts needed: just book a spot. Every level is welcome.

Don’t visit. Belong.

Limited spots per date so the group feels like friends, not a tour.
